A flooded basement is among the most stressful things a homeowner can face. The source may be a burst pipe, a leaking water heater, seepage through the foundation, a sump pump that quit or drains overwhelmed by a heavy rain, and in each case a fast professional response is what limits mold, structural damage and ruined possessions. Mountain View Restoration provides 24/7 emergency basement water removal throughout Mountain View, using powerful extraction equipment and advanced drying technology to get your basement dry and safe as quickly as possible.

Drying a basement is its own challenge: the space is below ground level, air barely circulates and dampness lingers. Between poured concrete underfoot, concrete block on every side and wet soil outside, water has nowhere to go and evaporation on its own is slow. We know those conditions well, so our technicians arrive with truck-mounted extractors for high volumes, submersible pumps for depth, and air movers and dehumidifiers placed strategically to suit a below-grade space.

We also look for the underlying reason the basement took on water, because fixing the cause helps stop the next flood. Our technicians inspect the sump pump system, foundation drainage, window wells, wall penetrations and grading to identify what contributed. Our advice on permanent protection follows, covering options like interior drain systems, backup pump installation and waterproofing to keep water out in the future.

How to Prevent Basement Water Removal Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Covers are worth considering for window wells, which also need to stay free of debris
  • For seepage that keeps coming back, an interior French drain is worth a look
  • Clogged gutters dump water at the base of the house, so clear them out each season
  • Every quarter, pour a bucket of water into the sump pit to verify the pump kicks on
  • Roof water should leave the downspouts no closer than 6 feet to the house foundation
  • Seal visible cracks in basement walls and floors with hydraulic cement

What a Professional Brings to Basement Water Removal

Basement water removal takes specialized equipment and expertise beyond what consumer pumps and fans can accomplish. Our submersible pumps move water much faster than anything you can rent, while commercial dryers control humidity, airflow and temperature, the conditions a below-grade structure needs because it resists evaporation on its own. When drying is not done properly, residual water in the framing, the insulation and the concrete ends in mold and material decay.

Basements also present their own safety hazards during a flood. Electrocution is possible whenever water reaches or covers outlets, wiring, the panel or plugged-in appliances. Flooding can also compromise the furnace, the water heater and natural gas lines. We are trained to size up and control each hazard first, then extract, so that everyone in the home and on our crew stays safe during restoration.

When to Call for Basement Water Removal in Mountain View

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water seeping through floor cracks, wall joints or around pipe penetrations
  • A damp, mildew smell that meets you on the basement stairs
  • Corrosion at the bottom of appliances, shelving legs or steel columns
  • Damp or wet walls, especially along the lower part of the foundation
  • Leaks at the window frames below grade, or wells holding water outside them
  • Basement flooring, carpet or tile that is discolored or breaking down
  • Finished walls downstairs with bubbled paint or drywall that crumbles
